What is Vocably?

Vocably is designed to help you use a new language in everyday life, because everyday use is the best and most natural form of learning.

Vocably is a browser extension and a mobile app. It works like a dictionary. You can use it to translate and learn every new word you see or hear in your life.

Every time you encounter a new English word, try translating it with Vocably and adding it to your collection. Review your words at least once a day, and within two or three months you'll see a real improvement in your English.

Not sure where to start with everyday language? Here are some ideas:

  • Read the news in English. BBC News, The Guardian and The Independent are all good places to start.
  • Make a habit of visiting British websites. Sites like MoneySavingExpert and Reddit (r/CasualUK) offer plenty of interesting content on all kinds of topics.
  • Watch British YouTube channels. For example, @TomScottGo — he makes fascinating videos in clear, easy-to-follow British English.

Do you want to improve faster? Change the language of your AI assistant to English. I wrote a guide about this. You can read it here: https://vocably.pro/ai.html. Give it a month or two, and you'll be surprised by the results.
