Vocably ni iki?

Vocably yateguwe kugira ngo igufashe gukoresha ururimi rushya mu buzima bwa buri munsi, kuko kurukoresha buri munsi ari bwo buryo bwiza kandi busanzwe bwo kwiga.

Vocably ni umushakisha na porogaramu ya telefoni. Ikora nk’inkoranyamagambo. Ushobora kurikoresha mu guhindura no kwiga buri jambo rishya ubona cyangwa wumva mu buzima.

Igihe cyose uhuye n’ijambo rishya ry’Ikinyarwanda, gerageza kurihindura ukoresheje Vocably no kuryongera mu cyegeranyo cyawe. Subiramo amagambo yawe nibura rimwe ku munsi, kandi mu mezi abiri cyangwa atatu uzabona iterambere nyaryo mu Kinyarwanda cyawe.
